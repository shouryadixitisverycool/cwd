On the paper itself

Cheat sheet (page 1 of the 2024 and 2025 papers; nothing else is given)
Gaussian N(μ, σ²)f(x) = 1⁄√(2πσ²) e−(x−μ)²/2σ², x ∈ ℝ
Poisson(λ)P(X = k) = λke−λ / k!, k = 0, 1, 2, …
Gamma(α, β)f(x) = βα⁄Γ(α) xα−1e−βx, x > 0, Γ(α) = ∫0∞ tα−1e−t dt
Memorise the rest yourself: the PMF/PDF, mean, variance and MGF of Bernoulli, Binomial, Geometric, Uniform, Exponential and Beta.

Format: 100 marks, 10–12 questions of 5–15 marks. The 2023 paper says "state reasons wherever applicable; precise mathematical arguments, no speeches."

What gets asked

  • Every year
    • MLE, 2–3 questions: Gaussian, Poisson, and Uniform[0,a] / [a,b] (maximum on the boundary, not where the derivative is 0).
    • Bias / MSE of an estimator.
    • Conjugate prior: posterior (7 marks) + MAP (3 marks). Beta–Bernoulli, Gamma–Poisson, Gamma–Exponential.
    • MGF derivation with its domain of t (10 marks).
    • Convergence proof from the definitions: in probability, in distribution, mean square, a.s. via Borel–Cantelli.
  • Often (2 of 3 years, 10 marks each): simulation proofs (accept–reject, Monte Carlo π) and Gaussian vectors / Jacobians.
  • Rarely or never: topics 1–3 on their own, numerical Chebyshev/CLT, covariance, total variance. Min/max and iterated expectation were asked once each.

What to expect this year

  • The 2026 surprise quiz (40 marks) confirms it: three inference questions (Uniform[a,b] MLE with b known plus its bias, a Laplace-scale MLE, a Beta–Geometric posterior) and the first regression question (MLEs of β* and σ² through the origin). All four are must-do in topics 10 and 11.
  • ~15–18 marks are freed up, since Markov chains are out of the syllabus. The likeliest places for them are inference and regression.
  • Questions repeat word for word (2024 → 2025: Uniform[a,b] MLE, Poisson MLE + MSE). Do every ★ must-do question.
  • Order: inference → MGFs → convergence → simulation + random vectors → regression → the rest.
  • Answer like the keys: state the support first, name the method, justify each step, give the MGF domain, box the result.
